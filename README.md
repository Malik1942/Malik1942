## 📱 The receipts (apps I've shipped)

**[Locant](https://locant.malikzhang.com)** · Point, don't describe. A free Mac app for people who build software with AI coding assistants like Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex. When a button or screen looks wrong, you usually describe it in words or paste a screenshot and hope the assistant guesses the right part of the code. With Locant you press a hotkey, point at the thing you mean (in a Mac app, an iPhone app in the Simulator, or a web page), and type what should change. The assistant gets that exact element: its name in the code, down to the source file and line, and for web pages the React component. In a blind test it edited the wrong copy of the code 0 times in 20 runs, against 14 in 20 with a screenshot, in about half the time and half the cost. Connects to the assistants in one click, open source, and your captures stay on your Mac. → [Repo](https://github.com/Malik1942/locant) · [Download](https://github.com/Malik1942/locant/releases/latest/download/Locant.dmg) · [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/locant)

**[Moti: Plan](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/moti-plan/id6770705491)** · An AI-native planner: feed it chaos, get back a living, timeline-aware plan. Designed and shipped solo in two weeks, with Claude and Codex on the build; SLM + LLM under the hood. → [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/moti-plan/id6770705491) · [Case study](https://www.malikzhang.com/project/moti)

**[Oryne](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oryne/id6778995892)** · Catch a thought by voice or text before it ghosts you, from your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, or a widget. On-device intelligence titles your Whispers, drifts related ones together into currents, brings old ones back when they matter again, and answers questions from your own notes. Everything syncs through your own private iCloud. Cloud AI exists now, but it stays off until you turn it on: privacy is the default, not a setting. Your 3am thoughts stay yours. → [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/oryne/id6778995892) · [Case study](https://www.malikzhang.com/project/oryne)

**[Inkwork](https://www.malikzhang.com/inkwork)** · A styled-QR studio with a point of view. Designed, built, and shipped solo. Go press the cube. It's satisfying.

**[CalmMouse](https://calmmouse.malikzhang.com)** · Magic Mouse fixes for macOS, born from pure daily rage: the page should not scroll every time you click. It grew from there: tap to click, per-app rules, smooth scrolling for wheel mice, and as of 1.3 a PS5 controller's touchpad as your mouse. Settings plays every option on a rendered, turnable Magic Mouse, so you see what a switch does before you flip it. Free, open source, installable via [Homebrew](https://github.com/Malik1942/homebrew-calmmouse). → [Repo](https://github.com/Malik1942/CalmMouse)

**[Tearsheet](https://tearsheet.malikzhang.com)** · Taste you can trace. Built at a Meta hackathon with Muse Spark: it reads the photos you already save (proportions, darkness, where one bright thing sits), turns them into design rules, then lays out product screens from them, every value traced back to a box on a photo. Type one sentence and it picks the photos and builds the screens; export tokens and a handoff doc when you like them. → [Repo](https://github.com/Malik1942/tearsheet) · [Video](https://youtu.be/_TyiYuu7dOM)

## 🤖 The AI workflow

Agents build with me, so I build for agents. It's a loop, and it compounds.

**Malik OS** · The operating system my agents run on, and the reason every app above got shipped instead of half-shipped. A kernel for how I work, skills for the design side (design engineering, PRDs, user research, storytelling, Figma conventions), agents with actual job titles (design reviewer, design engineer pair, user researcher, PM sparring partner), and a memory that means nobody re-explains me every Monday. Runs on its own runtime layer so the chassis can change without the judgment changing. Private, because it knows too much about me.

**[product-film](https://github.com/Malik1942/product-film)** · An agent skill that turns raw screen recordings into scored product films. AVFoundation pipeline, no ffmpeg, no editor, no tears.

**[screen-recording](https://github.com/Malik1942/screen-recording)** · Script-driven product capture across the iOS Simulator, websites, and Figma prototypes, with a staging protocol and measured-time auto-zoom. Works with Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor.

**[job-fit-screener](https://github.com/Malik1942/job-fit-screener)** · An agent skill that pulls live postings straight from companies' own Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever boards, drops the ones that fail your hard filters (quoting the line that failed), and gives apply / stretch / skip on the rest. Reads the body, not the title.

**[resume-tailor](https://github.com/Malik1942/resume-tailor)** · Its partner skill: interviews you once, then tailors your resume to one posting with every claim backed by your own evidence, renders the PDF, and checks that an applicant-tracking system can actually read it. Both work with Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor.

**[job-agent](https://github.com/Malik1942/job-agent)** · My job hunt, but as a pipeline: it scans ATS boards, scores roles, tailors cover letters, and applies, with an autonomy dial so I decide how much it does on its own. **How I run it now:** OpenClaw drives it from my phone, it scans, ranks, and fills every application in a parked browser, and I do the last step myself: read the form, fix what needs fixing, click submit. The agent never submits. No shell, allowlisted commands, at-most-once execution. Also private, for obvious reasons.
